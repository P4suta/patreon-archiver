# Usage restrictions

This tool is for personal offline archiving of content to which the user already has paid, authorized access.
It does not implement DRM bypass.
Widevine, FairPlay, PlayReady, and other protected streams are outside its scope.
It does not facilitate piracy, large-scale scraping, or redistribution of paid content.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable copyright law and its limitations, including the U.S.
DMCA, the EU Copyright Directive, and Japan's Copyright Act.
Users must also comply with the terms of every platform and publisher they access.
Those terms may restrict archiving even for paying subscribers.
Verify permission before use.

Do not redistribute archived content, in whole or in part, publicly or to third parties.
The software's MIT license grants no rights to videos, images, or other downloaded media.
Those rights remain with their creators and are subject to their terms.
